Avant Agro

Avant Agro is a premier supplier of authentic, farm-fresh spices cultivated in the fertile foothills and valleys of Northeast India. Sourced directly from local growers, our spices are renowned for their high essential oil content, intense aroma, and uncompromised purity.

Whether you require bulk wholesale consignments or customized commercial supply, our streamlined supply chain ensures reliable, timely delivery of the finest Northeast produce to any destination.

Our Northeast Spice Portfolio

Turmeric (Haldi)

High-curcumin Northeast varieties (including Lakadong & local grades), available as whole dry fingers or pure powder.

Ginger (Fresh & Dry)

Low-fiber, zesty Northeast ginger rich in natural oils and sharp, earthy aroma—ideal for wholesale and extraction.

Dry Chilli

Sun-dried Northeast red chillies (including fiery regional varieties & Bird's Eye), prized for natural pungency and deep color.

Cardamom (All Types)

Handpicked Green (Choti Elaichi) and Large Black (Badi Elaichi) cardamom pods from the misty hills of the Northeast.

Black Pepper

Bold, sun-cured black peppercorns with high piperine levels, delivering sharp heat and robust flavor.

Bay Leaf (Tejpat)

Naturally aromatic Northeast Indian bay leaves, carefully shade-dried and graded to retain volatile essential oils.

Long Pepper (Pipli / Lendi Pipal)

Wild-harvested and cultivated Northeast Pipali spikes, highly sought after for Ayurvedic, medicinal, and spice blends.

Cinnamon (Dalchini)

Sweet, woody, and richly fragrant bark quills harvested from mature Northeast cassia and cinnamon trees.

Star Anise

Whole, unbroken star-shaped pods picked at peak maturity from high-altitude orchards, rich in natural anethole.

Perilla Seeds (Nei Lieh)

Nutrient-dense, nutty indigenous Northeast oilseeds rich in Omega-3 fatty acids, cleaned for culinary and oil processing.

Zanthoxylum (Timbur / Sichuan Pepper)

Wild-grown highland berries prized for their citrusy aroma and signature tingling numbness in gourmet and medicinal applications.

Coriander (Dhania)

Crisp, golden-green whole coriander seeds grown in fertile river valleys, delivering a bright, citrus-warm profile.

Garlic (Lahsun)

Bold, high-allicin Northeast garlic bulbs cured and graded for superior pungency and extended transit shelf life.

Cumin (Jeera)

Machine-cleaned, aromatic cumin seeds packed with natural volatile oils for commercial spice processors and wholesalers.
